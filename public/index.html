How it works

What happens on a walk

If you can explain a job out loud, you can use Jefe.

01

Walk and talk

Tap record and talk. Jefe keeps listening with the phone in your pocket and writes down what matters.

“Front beds need mulch, call it three yards. Zone two head is broken, replace it.”

02

Fix the draft

What you get is a first draft. Tap a line to change a word, a quantity, or the price.

If Jefe missed something, it marks that line instead of guessing. You don't send a number you didn't choose.

03

Send the paperwork

Turn the notes into an estimate, an invoice, a work order, or a report, with your letterhead on it.

Text it, email it, or save the PDF before you leave the driveway.

Privacy

Your audio stays on your phone

Speech turns into text on the phone. Your voice is not uploaded. The typed words go to our AI provider so it can write the document, and they come back to your phone. Your work is not used to train AI models. Read the privacy policy

Control

You can change every line

What Jefe hears is a first draft. If it missed something, it marks that line instead of guessing. You don't send a number you didn't choose.
