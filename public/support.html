Questions

How much does Jefe cost?

After five free walks, Jefe is $29.99 a month or $299.99 a year. You cancel in your iPhone settings. You don't put a card down for the free walks.

Is there anything free?

The first five walks are free, and the download is free. You don't enter a card for those walks, and there is nothing to cancel.

Does Jefe run on Android?

Jefe runs on iPhone only right now. There isn't an Android version yet.

Does my audio leave my phone?

Your audio stays on your phone. Speech turns into text on the device. To write the paperwork, the typed words go to the AI provider and come back as the document. Your work is not used to train AI models. The details are on the privacy policy.
