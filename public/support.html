Questions

How much does Jefe cost?

After five free walks, Jefe is $39.99 a month or $299.99 a year. You cancel in your iPhone settings, the same way as anything else. No card is required for the free walks.

Is there anything free?

Your first five walks are free. No card, nothing to cancel. The app is free to download.

Does Jefe run on Android?

No. Jefe is iPhone only for now. There is no Android version yet.

Does my audio leave my phone?

Your audio never leaves your phone. Speech is turned into text on the device. To write the paperwork, the typed-out words go to the AI provider and come back as your document. Your work is not used to train AI models. The details are on the privacy policy.
