Mulch calculator

Cubic yards of mulch for a bed, from the area and a depth. This page starts at 3 inches, which is the usual depth in the Jefe app, and at 10% waste. Bagged mulch is counted in 2 cubic foot bags. Leave the price blank and you will not see a dollar amount.

Worked example

500 square feet at 3 inches, with the 10% waste this page starts from.

Raw volume is 125 cubic feet. With waste that is 137.5 cubic feet, or 5.09 cubic yards.

At 2 cubic feet a bag, that is 69 bags. No price was entered, so there is no dollar amount.

Formulas

  • Cubic feet = area in square feet × depth in feet.
  • With waste = cubic feet × (1 + waste ÷ 100).
  • Cubic yards = cubic feet with waste ÷ 27.
  • Bags = cubic feet with waste ÷ 2, rounded up.
  • A price per cubic yard, if you type one, is multiplied by the cubic yards after they are rounded to the hundredth. Leave it blank and no dollar amount is shown.

Questions

What depth does the mulch page start at?

Mulch starts at 3 inches. That is the usual depth in the Jefe app. Change it if this bed is different.

How are bags counted?

Bagged mulch, gravel, topsoil, and sand are counted as 2 cubic foot bags, rounded up. You can’t buy a fraction of a bag.

What if I leave the price blank?

You still get cubic yards and a bag count. No dollar amount is shown. Zero is treated the same as blank.
