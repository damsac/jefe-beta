Area and volume calculator

Measure a rectangle, a circle, a slab, or a cylinder. Lengths can be feet, inches, yards, or meters. Jefe converts them to feet, then reports square feet and, for a volume, cubic yards. This page does not add waste and does not price the job.

Calculator

Fill in the measurements to see a result.

Estimate only. Confirm quantities with your supplier and plans.

Worked example

A rectangle 12 ft by 16 ft is 192 square feet.

A circle 10 ft across is 78.54 square feet (π × 5²).

A slab 10 ft by 10 ft by 4 in is 33.33 cubic feet, which is 1.23 cubic yards.

Formulas

  • Rectangle area = length × width.
  • Circle area = π × (diameter ÷ 2)². Enter the diameter, not the radius.
  • Rectangle volume = length × width × depth.
  • Cylinder volume = π × (diameter ÷ 2)² × height.
  • Cubic yards = cubic feet ÷ 27.

Questions

What units can I enter?

Feet, inches, yards, or meters. They are converted to feet first: 12 inches to a foot, 3 feet to a yard, and one meter is 1/0.3048 feet.

Does this add waste?

No. Area and volume report the measurement only. Concrete and the material pages start at 10% waste, which you can change.

Why is there no price?

This page does not price a job. There is no dollar amount because none was entered, and a blank is not shown as $0.
