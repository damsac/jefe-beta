Sand calculator

Cubic yards of sand for a bed, from the area and the depth. Depth starts at 2 inches, the usual depth in the Jefe app, and waste starts at 10%. Bagged sand is counted in 2 cubic foot bags. Leave the price blank and you will not see a dollar amount.

Worked example

80 square feet at 2 inches, with 10% waste. Raw volume is 13.33 cubic feet. With waste the order is 0.54 cubic yards, or 8 bags of 2 cubic feet.

No price was entered, so there is no dollar amount.

Formulas

  • Cubic feet = area in square feet × depth in feet.
  • With waste = cubic feet × (1 + waste ÷ 100).
  • Cubic yards = cubic feet with waste ÷ 27.
  • Bags = cubic feet with waste ÷ 2, rounded up.
  • A price per cubic yard, if you type one, is multiplied by the cubic yards after they are rounded to the hundredth. Leave it blank and no dollar amount is shown.

Questions

What depth does the sand page start at?

Sand starts at 2 inches. That is the usual depth in the Jefe app. Change it if this bed is different.

How are bags counted?

Bagged mulch, gravel, topsoil, and sand are counted as 2 cubic foot bags, rounded up. You can’t buy a fraction of a bag.

What if I leave the price blank?

You still get cubic yards and a bag count. No dollar amount is shown. Zero is treated the same as blank.
