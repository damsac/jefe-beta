How it works

Three steps. No typing.

If you can talk through a job, you can use Jefe.

01

Walk & talk

Tap once and talk. Jefe listens the whole time and writes down what matters — even with the phone in your pocket.

“Front beds need mulch, call it three yards… zone two head is broken, replace it.”

02

Fix anything

It's a first draft, not a verdict. Tap any line to fix a word, change a quantity, or put your price on it.

Jefe flags what it didn't hear, so nothing gets quoted by accident.

03

Send it

Turn it into an estimate, invoice, work order or report with your letterhead on it.

Text it, email it, or save the PDF before you leave the driveway.

Privacy

Your audio never leaves your phone

The listening happens on your device — your voice is never uploaded. To write the paperwork, the typed-out words go to our AI provider and come back as your document. Your work is never used to train AI models. How it works ›

Control

You're in control of every word

What Jefe hears is a first draft, and every line of it is yours to change. It marks the things it didn't catch instead of guessing, so you are never sending a number you didn't choose.

Five walks free, then decide
